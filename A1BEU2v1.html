1. Vocabulary Presentation

Company Information

  • • Company = a business organization
  • • Name = what the company is called
  • • Location = where the company is (city, country)
  • • Address = the street address of the company
  • • Headquarters = the main office of a company

What Companies Do

  • • Products = things a company makes or sells
  • • Services = help or work a company provides
  • • Business = the work a company does
  • • Industry = the type of business (e.g., technology)
  • • Market = the customers who buy products

People in Companies

  • • Employees = people who work for the company
  • • Staff = all the people who work for the company
  • • Workers = people who do jobs in the company
  • • Manager = a person who leads other employees
  • • CEO = the top person in a company

Company Size and Success

  • • Small company = few employees (1-50)
  • • Large company = many employees (1000+)
  • • Successful = doing well, making money
  • • International = works in many countries
  • • Local = works in one area or city

2. Reading

Company Profile Examples

Profile 1: TechSolutions Inc.

TechSolutions Inc. is a technology company. The company is located in San Francisco, California, USA. TechSolutions has 250 employees and makes software products for small businesses. The company provides IT services to help other companies with their computers. TechSolutions is successful and works with clients in the United States and Canada.

Profile 2: Green Foods Ltd.

Green Foods Ltd. is a food company in London, England. The company has 45 employees and makes organic food products. Green Foods sells healthy snacks, drinks, and meals to local supermarkets. The headquarters are in central London. This small company started in 2010 and is growing every year.

Profile 3: Global Transport

Global Transport is an international shipping company. The company headquarters are in Tokyo, Japan, but they have offices in 15 countries. Global Transport has 5,000 employees worldwide. The company provides shipping services to move products from one country to another. They work with many large companies in different industries.

3. Grammar Presentation

Basic Grammar Structures

Pattern 1: Subject + is + Information

  • The company is successful.
  • The company is located in London.
  • The company is international.

Pattern 2: Subject + has + Noun

  • The company has 100 employees.
  • It has offices in 5 countries.

Pattern 3: Subject + Verb + Noun

  • The company makes cars.
  • The company provides services.
  • The company sells food.

4. Practical Task: Writing

Writing Task: Create a Company Profile

Step 1 & 2: Plan Your Company

Fill in this information for your imaginary company.

Step 3: Write Your 5-Sentence Profile

Use your plan and this structure to write your profile below.

  • Sentence 1: [Company name] is a [industry] company.
  • Sentence 2: The company is located in [city, country].
  • Sentence 3: It has [number] employees.
  • Sentence 4: It makes/provides [products/services].
  • Sentence 5: The company is [successful/growing/etc.].
